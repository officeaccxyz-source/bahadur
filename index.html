GOOD TO KNOW / CLIENT QUESTIONS

Before we
get started.

How does a bespoke appointment work?

We discuss your occasion, style and fit preferences, then guide you through cloth selection, measurements and fitting steps.

Can I book an online consultation from overseas?

Yes. Choose Online consultation in the appointment request and our team will contact you to arrange the next step.

How long does tailoring take?

Timing depends on the garment, cloth and fitting schedule. We confirm an estimated timeline after understanding your commission.

Do you publish fixed prices?

Prices depend on garment type, cloth and design details. Request a consultation for a tailored quotation.

Is my appointment confirmed immediately?

No. Your request is received first. Our team must confirm availability before the appointment is booked.
